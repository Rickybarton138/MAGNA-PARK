4. Keep a browser tab open for that email inbox — you will get many verification emails

**Submission order (most impactful first):**

1. **Maps first** — Apple Business Connect, Bing Places (import from GBP), Waze

3. **Storage-specific** — Self Storage Finder, Compare The Storage, Storage.co.uk, Self Storage Search, Storage Locator

- Bing Places lets you import your Google Business Profile directly — use this to save time
- NAP consistency is critical: always use the exact same Name, Address, Phone format across every listing
- Batch the verification emails — open all confirmation links in one session
- Budget ~2-3 hours total for all free submissions; paid memberships (chambers, Compare The Storage) can wait
