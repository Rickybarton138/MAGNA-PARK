Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

In short: We only collect the information you give us when requesting a quote. We don't sell your data, and we only use it to respond to your enquiry and provide our storage services.

1. Who We Are

Magna Park Self Storage is operated by the Astra Group. We provide shipping container storage services at 106 Provence Dr, Bournemouth, BH11 9FA.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at:

  • Email: magnaparkselfstore@outlook.com
  • Phone: 01202 113255

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when:

  • Submitting a quote request through our website
  • Contacting us by phone or email
  • Entering into a storage rental agreement

This information may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Details about your storage requirements

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as health information, religious beliefs, or financial details through our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and quote requests
  • Provide our storage services
  • Communicate with you about your storage rental
  • Send important service updates

We do not: Sell your personal information to third parties, send unsolicited marketing emails, or share your data with unrelated businesses.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we process your data based on:

  • Consent: When you submit a quote request, you consent to us using your details to respond
  • Contract: When you rent storage from us, we need your details to fulfil our agreement
  • Legitimate interest: To respond to enquiries and provide customer service

5. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as necessary to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Provide ongoing services if you become a customer
  • Comply with legal obligations

Quote requests from non-customers are deleted after 12 months. Customer records are retained for 6 years after the end of your rental agreement for legal and accounting purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access – request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification – ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure – ask us to delete your data (where applicable)
  • Restriction – ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability – request your data in a portable format
  • Object – object to certain types of processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.
