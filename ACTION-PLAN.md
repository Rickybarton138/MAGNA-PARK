## Action 1: Submit Sitemap in Search Console (30 seconds) ✅ DONE 2026-04-20

**Why:** Google has only indexed 2 of your 33 pages. Until the sitemap is submitted, 31 pages are invisible. This is the single biggest unlock.

**Steps:**

1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console
2. You should see your property `https://www.magnaparkselfstore.co.uk/` in the dashboard
3. In the left menu, click **Sitemaps**
4. In the "Add a new sitemap" field, type: `sitemap.xml`
5. Click **Submit**
6. You should see a green "Success" confirmation
7. It will say "Discovered URLs" — should show 33

**If it fails or shows 0 URLs:**
- The sitemap uses non-www URLs but your SC property is www. You may need to add a second property:
- Click the property dropdown (top left) → **Add property** → URL prefix → `https://magnaparkselfstore.co.uk` (no www)
- Verify using the HTML file method (google0f5f5cc1b247f8c4.html is already on your site)
- Submit `sitemap.xml` on this new property too

**How to know it worked:** Check back in 24-48 hours. Go to Coverage (or Pages) in the left menu. You should see indexed pages climbing from 2 toward 33 over the next week.

## Action 2: Text 10+ Customers the Review Link (15 minutes)

**Why:** Zero Google reviews means you can't enter the local map pack (the 3-business section at the top of search results). Reviews are the #1 ranking factor for local search. 10 reviews puts you ahead of most independents.

**The link to send:** `https://magnaparkselfstore.co.uk/reviews.html`

**Text message template — copy and personalise:**

```
Hi [name], it's Ricky from Magna Park Self Store. Quick favour — would you mind leaving us a Google review? Takes 30 seconds and it genuinely helps us as a small local business. Here's the link: https://magnaparkselfstore.co.uk/reviews.html   Cheers, Ricky
```

**Who to text:**
- Every current customer with a container
- Every past customer you're still in contact with
- Any friends/family who've visited the site or helped with the setup
- Any tradespeople who've done work at the site

**Tips:**
- Send from your personal phone, not a business number — people respond to personal messages
- Personalise the name — "Hi Dave" not "Hi customer"
- Don't send more than 10 in one batch (WhatsApp/SMS spam filters)
- If they say "sure, what do I write?" tell them: "Just say what you stored and how you found it — doesn't need to be long, even one sentence helps"
- The best time to ask: right after they've loaded their container and you've helped them. That's when goodwill is highest.

**Target:**
- 10 reviews in week 1
- 2-3 reviews per week ongoing (ask every new customer on day 1)

## Action 6: Shoot 10 Site Photos for GBP (15 minutes at the site)

**Why:** GBP listings with 10+ photos get 42% more direction requests and 35% more website clicks (Google's own data). Your listing currently has minimal photos. The foam-insulated interior is your visual USP and needs to be prominent.

**Equipment needed:** Just your phone. No special gear.

**Before you go:**
- Enable location services on your camera: iPhone → Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Camera → While Using. Android → Camera app → Settings → Save location → On.
- Clean out one container so the foam lining is clearly visible
- Park your car/van next to a container for the "drive-up" shot

**Shot list (shoot in this order):**

| # | Photo | How to frame it | Why |
|---|-------|-----------------|-----|
| 1 | **Site entrance with signage** | Stand across the road. Wide landscape. Signage in centre. | This becomes your Google Maps cover photo |
| 2 | **Container interior — foam lining** | Stand inside, shoot towards the open door. Foam walls visible on all sides. Natural daylight. | **This is your money shot.** Your entire USP in one image |
| 3 | **Container exterior — showing 20ft size + lock** | Step back 3-4 metres. Shoot at a slight angle so you see the length + the door/lock | Shows the product |
| 4 | **Drive-up access** | Position car/van next to a container, tailgate open. Shoot from behind the vehicle looking at the container | Proves the "drive right up" promise |
| 5 | **Gate / perimeter fencing** | Wide shot showing the full fence line with the gate | Security signal for nervous customers |
| 6 | **CCTV camera close-up** | Zoom in on a mounted CCTV camera with the site behind it blurred | Security signal |
| 7 | **Aerial / wide site shot** | If you have any high ground nearby, or lean out of an upper window. Otherwise, stand at the far corner and shoot the full row of containers | Shows scale and professionalism |
| 8 | **Magna Park signage close-up** | Tight frame on the sign. Make sure it's in focus and readable. | Brand recognition |
| 9 | **Container with door open showing capacity** | Front-on, daylight flooding in. If you can put a few boxes/furniture inside to show scale, even better | Helps customers visualise "will my stuff fit?" |
| 10 | **You (or your team) at the site** | Smile. Stand by a container. Casual but professional. | Humanises the listing — people trust people |

**Rules:**
- Landscape orientation for shots 1, 4, 5, 7. Portrait OK for 2, 3, 6, 8.
- Natural daylight between 10am-3pm. Avoid shadows.
- No filters, no editing apps. Google's AI downranks obviously edited photos.
- No people without their consent. The "you at the site" shot is the exception.

**Upload to GBP:**
1. Go to business.google.com → Photos
2. Upload each photo
3. Set photo 1 as **Cover photo**
4. Upload your logo (`MAGNA PARK LOGO.PNG`) as **Logo** if you haven't already
5. Categorise each photo: Exterior, Interior, or Team
