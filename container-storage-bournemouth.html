Shipping Container Storage That Actually Protects Your Stuff

Standard shipping containers are tough on the outside, but basic steel boxes have a well-known weakness: condensation. As temperatures fluctuate — particularly near the Dorset coast — moisture builds up inside uninsulated metal walls and drips onto your belongings. That's why every container at Magna Park is lined with foam insulation, creating a stable internal environment that guards against damp, mould, and temperature damage.

The site is fully fenced, gated, and monitored by CCTV around the clock.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between container storage and self storage?

Traditional self storage uses indoor rooms in a warehouse. Container storage uses steel shipping containers on an outdoor site. Our containers are foam insulated, so you get the security and space of a container with protection from the elements.

Are your containers watertight?

Yes. Our 20ft shipping containers are fully watertight and wind-proof. The added foam insulation also prevents internal condensation — the most common cause of damage in container storage.

Can I drive a van right up to the container?

Absolutely. Our site has wide access roads and each container has direct drive-up access. You can reverse a van or trailer right to the doors.

What if I only need half a container?

We offer full 20ft containers only. However, at £55/week with no VAT, our price is often cheaper than a small room at a traditional storage facility, and you'll have space to spare.
