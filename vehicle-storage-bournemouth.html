Frequently Asked Questions

Will my car fit in a 20ft container?

Most standard cars fit comfortably. A 20ft container is 20ft long and 8ft wide internally. Vehicles up to about 17ft long and 7ft wide will fit with room to spare. Large SUVs and long-wheelbase vans should check exact dimensions.

Is container storage bad for cars?

Uninsulated containers can be, because of condensation. Our foam insulated containers prevent this, maintaining a drier internal environment. We recommend a breathable car cover for extra protection and ensuring the vehicle is clean and dry before storage.

Can I store a motorbike and a car together?

Yes, if they both fit within the 20ft x 8ft footprint. Many customers store a car plus one or two motorbikes in the same container.

Do I need to notify my insurer?

You should inform your vehicle insurer that the car is in storage. Many insurers offer reduced premiums for vehicles kept in a locked, secure compound with CCTV — which our site provides.
