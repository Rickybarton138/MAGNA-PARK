Making Your Bournemouth House Move Easier

House moves rarely go to plan. Completion dates shift, chains break, and there's often a gap between moving out of one property and into the next. That gap is stressful enough without worrying about where to put everything you own. Magna Park Self Storage solves the problem with a simple, affordable solution: a 20ft foam insulated container that holds your entire household, available on flexible weekly terms.

Our containers are sized for house moves. At 1,360 cubic feet, a single 20ft container comfortably holds the contents of a three-bedroom house — furniture, appliances, boxes, the lot. For larger properties, two containers side by side give you ample room. The site on Provence Drive in BH11 is centrally located for moves across Bournemouth, and removal companies regularly deliver to our site. Wide access roads and drive-up loading make professional removal teams' jobs much easier.

Timing is everything with house moves, and our flexible weekly terms are designed for exactly this situation. Start your rental the week you move out, and end it the week you move in. No monthly minimums, no penalty for short stays, no requirement to give months of notice. Many of our house move customers store for 2-6 weeks — at £55/week, the total cost is a fraction of what chain facilities charge for the same period.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do most people store during a house move?

Typically 2-6 weeks. Some customers store for longer if there's a gap in their property chain. Our weekly terms mean you only pay for what you use — there's no monthly minimum.

Can my removal company load the container?

Yes. Give them our address (106 Provence Dr, BH11 9FA) and they can drive straight to the container. Many Bournemouth removal firms already know our site.

Should I book in advance for a house move?

We recommend contacting us as soon as you know your moving date. Containers are subject to availability, and house move season (spring/summer) is busier. Call 01202 113255 to check availability.

What if my completion date changes at the last minute?

No problem. Our flexible weekly terms mean there's no fixed end date. If your move-in is delayed, you simply keep the container for extra weeks at £55/week. No penalty, no rebooking fee.
