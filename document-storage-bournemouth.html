Frequently Asked Questions

How many archive boxes fit in a 20ft container?

A standard archive box is approximately 1.5 cubic feet. A 20ft container holds 1,360 cubic feet, so theoretically around 900 boxes — though practical stacking and access aisles reduce this to 500-700 boxes depending on your organisation system.

Is a container suitable for confidential documents?

Yes. Each container is locked with your own padlock (you keep the only key), constructed from heavy-gauge steel, and on a site with 24/7 CCTV and pin code access. For GDPR purposes, containers meet the requirements for secure storage of personal data in physical form.

Will paper documents get damp?

Not in our insulated containers. The foam lining prevents condensation, which is the main cause of moisture damage in metal storage. We recommend using plastic archive boxes or liners for extra protection of particularly sensitive documents.

Can I organise the container with shelving?

Yes. Many document storage customers install freestanding shelving inside the container to organise archive boxes and create easy access. You're welcome to fit shelving as long as it's freestanding and removed when you leave.
