# Removal Company Partnership Programme

**Objective:** Build a referral pipeline with local removal companies. Storage is a natural adjacent service — removers lose jobs when clients need mid-move storage and they can't offer it. Magna Park wins those jobs and pays a referral fee back.

**Offer to them:**
- £20 referral fee per new customer (cash, Amazon voucher, or fuel card — their choice)
- OR 10% of the first month's rent (£22 equivalent on our weekly rate)
- Exclusive "preferred remover" listing on our website (if high volume)
- Reciprocal referrals back to them when our customers need a mover

## Outreach Cadence

**Week 1:** Send the email below to all 14 Tier 1 companies. Log responses in a simple spreadsheet (Name / Contacted / Replied / Meeting / Signed Up).

**Week 2:** Follow up by phone to any Tier 1 non-responders. Removers answer their phones — they're field-based.

**Week 3:** Send the Tier 2 (overflow partner) variant to the remaining 6.

**Week 4:** In-person visit to the top 3 warmest responses. Take a bacon sandwich, tour them round the site, seal the deal face-to-face.

## Phone script (if email doesn't work)

> "Hi, is that [name]? It's Ricky from Magna Park Self Storage on Provence Drive — are you in the middle of a job or is it an alright time for two minutes?
>
> Quick one — I sent you an email about referral fees for storage customers, didn't know if you'd had a chance to look. Basically £20 for every person you send us, no contract, no faff. Any interest in a quick chat or shall I pop round with a brew when you're next in the yard?"

Keep it short, friendly, and assume they're busy. Most removers will appreciate that you're not wasting their time.
