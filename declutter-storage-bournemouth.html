Declutter Your Home Without the Difficult Decisions

Decluttering is supposed to feel liberating, but the hardest part is deciding what to get rid of. The children's old toys, your parents' furniture, seasonal decorations, that exercise bike you'll definitely use again — these items have value, whether financial or sentimental, but they're taking up space in your Bournemouth home. A container at Magna Park gives you a middle ground: clear the space now, and decide what to keep or sell later.

This approach is particularly useful for downsizing. If you're moving from a larger family home in Boscombe or Westbourne to a smaller property or retirement flat, you simply can't fit everything. Rather than making rushed decisions during the stress of a move, store the overflow and sort through it at your leisure over the coming weeks or months. At £55/week with no VAT, it's an affordable buffer that removes the time pressure.

Our 20ft containers hold 1,360 cubic feet, which is far more than most decluttering projects produce. You'll have room to organise items clearly — keep boxes on one side, furniture on the other, with a walkway down the middle. The foam insulation protects everything from condensation, so sentimental items, photo albums, books, and clothing stay in the same condition as when you stored them. And with 24/7 access, you can gradually sort through items whenever the mood takes you.

Frequently Asked Questions

I'm downsizing from a 4-bed to a 2-bed — will one container be enough?

Probably yes. A 20ft container holds the contents of a three-bedroom house. The excess from a 4-bed downsized to a 2-bed should fit comfortably, with room to organise and access items.

Can I use the container as a staging area for selling items?

Yes. Many decluttering customers use their container as a base for sorting items to sell on eBay, Facebook Marketplace, or at car boot sales. 24/7 access means you can photograph and list items on your own schedule.

How long do downsizing customers typically store for?

Typically 2-6 months. Some customers keep a container longer as permanent overflow storage. Our flexible weekly terms accommodate any duration.

Is it worth storing items or should I just donate them?

That depends on the items. A container at £55/week buys you time to make considered decisions rather than rushing to donate or bin things you might regret. Many customers find they sell enough stored items to cover the storage costs.
