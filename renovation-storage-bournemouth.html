Keep Your Belongings Safe While You Renovate

Home renovations in Bournemouth — whether you're extending a Victorian terrace in Winton, converting a loft in Moordown, or gutting a kitchen in Southbourne — mean living in a building site. Dust gets everywhere, paint splatters travel further than you'd think, and heavy tools and materials need space that's currently occupied by your furniture. The smartest move is to clear the rooms being worked on and store everything safely off-site.

At Magna Park, a single 20ft container holds the contents of a three-bedroom house. For most renovations, you'll only need to store the contents of one or two rooms, meaning there's plenty of space for everything with room to organise. The foam insulation keeps your furniture protected from condensation during what might be a storage period of several weeks or months. Drive-up access on our Provence Drive site makes loading and unloading straightforward — your builder can even help load on their way to the tip.

Renovation timelines are notoriously unpredictable. Builders overrun, materials get delayed, and what was supposed to be a three-week job stretches into three months. Our flexible weekly terms at £55/week with no VAT mean this doesn't cost you a fortune. There's no fixed end date, no monthly minimum, and no penalty for extending. Your furniture stays safe for as long as the renovation takes, and you can dip in any time (24/7 access) to grab things you need.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do most renovation storage customers stay?

Typically 4-12 weeks, though some larger projects run longer. Our weekly terms mean you're never locked in — pay for what you use.

Should I store everything or just items from the rooms being worked on?

That depends on the scope of your renovation. For a kitchen or bathroom refit, usually just that room's contents. For a full house renovation, most customers clear everything for the builders' convenience. A 20ft container has room for it all.

Can my builder access the container to get my things?

You control access with your own padlock. If you want your builder to access the container, you'd need to provide them with a key to your padlock and the site pin code.

What about storing items that are already dusty from building work?

We recommend cleaning items before storage to prevent dust from spreading. Cover furniture with dust sheets in the container. The sealed environment of our containers means no additional dust will get in once the doors are shut.
