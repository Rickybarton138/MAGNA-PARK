Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum rental period?

One week. There is no minimum term beyond that. If you need a container for just one or two weeks, that's perfectly fine.

Do I need to give notice to leave?

Yes, we ask for reasonable notice so we can prepare the container for the next customer. Contact us when you're ready to end your rental.

Is short-term storage more expensive per week?

No. Our rate is £55/week regardless of how long you stay. Short-term and long-term customers pay the same weekly rate.

Can I extend my rental if plans change?

Absolutely. If you initially planned for a few weeks but need longer, simply keep your container. There's no need to re-book or sign a new agreement. Just continue paying weekly.
