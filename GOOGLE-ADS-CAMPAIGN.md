# Google Ads Campaign — Magna Park Self Storage

**Objective:** Drive qualified enquiries for 20ft insulated container storage in Bournemouth / Poole / Dorset. Target CPL: £15-£25. Target conversion: a quote form submit or phone call.

**Recommended starting budget:** £15/day (£450/month). Scale up when you're consistently below £25/lead.

---

## Campaign Structure (2 campaigns, 6 ad groups)

### Campaign 1: Search — Intent
- **Type:** Search
- **Bid strategy:** Maximise Conversions (once you have 15+ conversions; start on Manual CPC for first 2 weeks)
- **Budget:** £10/day
- **Languages:** English
- **Locations:** Bournemouth, Poole, Christchurch, Wimborne, Ferndown, Ringwood, Verwood, Burton, New Milton, Southbourne — **+5 mile radius around BH11 9FA**
- **Location targeting:** People IN or regularly in target locations (not "interested in")
- **Ad schedule:** All days, 06:00–23:00 (cheaper clicks, avoids dead hours)
- **Device bid adjustments:** Mobile +20% (most storage searches are mobile)

### Campaign 2: Search — Competitor + Brand Defence
- **Type:** Search
- **Bid strategy:** Manual CPC
- **Budget:** £5/day
- **Same locations as above**
- **Purpose:** Bid on "Big Yellow Bournemouth", "Safestore Poole" etc. and show your price comparison. Also protect "Magna Park" brand searches.

## Conversion Tracking Setup

**Before going live — REQUIRED.** Without conversion tracking, Google can't optimise.

1. **Install Google Tag (gtag.js)** on all pages — goes in `<head>`:
   ```html
   <!-- Google tag (gtag.js) -->
   <script async src="https://www.googletagmanager.com/gtag/js?id=AW-XXXXXXXXX"></script>
   <script>
     window.dataLayer = window.dataLayer || [];
     function gtag(){dataLayer.push(arguments);}
     gtag('js', new Date());
     gtag('config', 'AW-XXXXXXXXX');
   </script>
   ```

2. **Set up conversions in Google Ads:**
   - Conversion 1: **Quote form submit** → fires on thank-you.html page load (primary conversion)
   - Conversion 2: **Phone call from ads** → Google Ads auto-generates a call forwarding number
   - Conversion 3: **Phone click on mobile** → fires when `tel:` link is tapped (secondary)

3. **Thank-you page conversion snippet** (add to `thank-you.html` head):
   ```html
   <script>
     gtag('event', 'conversion', {
       'send_to': 'AW-XXXXXXXXX/YYYYYYYYYYY',
       'value': 1.0,
       'currency': 'GBP'
     });
   </script>
   ```

4. **Link Google Analytics 4** to Google Ads if you have GA4 running.

## Things that will blow your budget — avoid

- **Broad match keywords** (without a bid strategy that's learning) — Google will show your ad for "storage units definition"
- **No negative keywords** — you'll pay for "free storage ideas" clicks
- **No conversion tracking** — Google optimises for clicks not customers, you burn money
- **Targeting "interested in" locations** — you'll get London clicks
- **Sending ads to homepage** — use specific landing pages for each ad group
- **Not using call extensions** — 40% of storage enquiries are phone calls, you lose them if the number isn't in the ad
