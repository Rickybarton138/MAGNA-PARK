## Bournemouth's first foam-insulated container storage facility offers self storage at half the price of national chains

**Bournemouth, Dorset** — A new self storage facility in Bournemouth is offering residents and businesses 20ft shipping container storage at £55 per week with no VAT — roughly half the price of national chains like Big Yellow and Safestore for comparable space.

Magna Park Self Store Ltd, based at 106 Provence Dr (BH11 9FA), is the only storage facility in the Bournemouth area to offer fully foam-insulated containers. Unlike standard shipping containers used by most storage yards, every unit at Magna Park is lined with closed-cell foam insulation, which prevents the condensation that commonly damages furniture, clothing, documents and electronics in uninsulated metal containers.

"Most people don't realise that a standard shipping container sweats," said Ricky Barton, owner of Magna Park Self Store. "When the temperature drops at night, moisture forms on the inside of the metal walls and drips onto whatever you've stored. Our foam lining stops that completely — your belongings stay dry year-round."

Each 20ft container provides 1,360 cubic feet of storage — enough to fit the entire contents of a typical three-bedroom house. All customers receive a personal PIN code for 24/7 drive-up access, with CCTV monitoring and secure gated entry as standard.

The facility serves customers across Bournemouth, Poole, Christchurch, Wimborne, Ferndown and the wider Dorset area, and is proving popular with a range of users including families between house moves, local businesses storing stock and equipment, students from Bournemouth University and Arts University Bournemouth, and classic car owners seeking climate-stable protection.

"We wanted to offer something that the big chains don't — proper insulation, simple weekly pricing with no VAT, and no long contracts," Barton added. "You rent by the week, you get your own container, and you can drive right up to it at any time of day or night."

Magna Park Self Store is now accepting bookings. For more information or a free quote, visit [www.magnaparkselfstore.co.uk](https://magnaparkselfstore.co.uk) or call 01202 113255.
